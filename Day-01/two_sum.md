# Two Sum

- **Problem:** Two Sum
- **Platform:** LeetCode
- **Problem Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach
I used two nested loops to check every pair of elements in the array. If the sum of two elements equals the target, their indices are printed.

## Time Complexity
O(n²)

## Space Complexity
O(1)

## Test Case
- Array: [2, 7, 11, 15]
- Target: 9
- Output: Indices 0 and 1
